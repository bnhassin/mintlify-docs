The personalised chat pairs a conversational assistant with a live board preview, so you can shape ideas in chat and see them rendered on a board in real time. This page covers the Creative Prompt dashboard metric and the features that power the experience.

## Creative Prompt metric

The Creative Prompt metric surfaces on your dashboard and tracks how often personalised chat sessions generate a board preview.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Prompts started" icon="play">
    Total number of personalised chat sessions initiated from a Creative Prompt.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Previews rendered" icon="eye">
    How many sessions produced a live board preview alongside the chat.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Boards saved" icon="floppy-disk">
    Sessions where the user saved the generated board to their workspace.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Prompt-to-board rate" icon="chart-line">
    Ratio of prompts that resulted in a saved board. A quick signal of prompt quality.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Features overview

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Personalised responses" icon="user">
    Chat replies adapt to your account context: recent boards, saved themes, and previous prompts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Live board preview" icon="chalkboard">
    Every chat turn updates a board preview panel so you can see the result take shape as you iterate.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Prompt suggestions" icon="lightbulb">
    Starter prompts and follow-ups are suggested based on the board type you're building.
  </Card>
  <Card title="One-click save" icon="bookmark">
    Save the current preview to your workspace at any point in the conversation.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Sign in and open the dashboard. The Creative Prompt metric card appears at the top of the overview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch a Creative Prompt">
    Click **Start a prompt** on the metric card to open the personalised chat with the board preview panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Iterate in chat">
    Describe what you want. The board preview updates with each response so you can refine tone, layout, and content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your board">
    When you're happy with the preview, click **Save board** to add it to your workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The Creative Prompt metric refreshes hourly. Recent sessions may take a few minutes to appear in the dashboard totals.
</Note>
